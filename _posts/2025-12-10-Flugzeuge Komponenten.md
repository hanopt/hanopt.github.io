✅ 1. Static Ports (center-right, inside the red outline)

These small holes labeled F/O (First Officer) and CAPT (Captain) are static ports.
They measure static air pressure, which is used for:

Altitude (altimeter)

Vertical speed (VSI)

Airspeed indicator (together with pitot tubes)

The red box around them says:

“STATIC PORTS – DO NOT PLUG OR DEFORM HOLES. Area within red line must be smooth and clean.”

This is crucial, because blocking static ports has caused fatal aircraft accidents (e.g., Birgenair 301).

✅ 2. Avionics Equipment Vent / Air Inlet Valve (left side)

The round panel labeled AVIONICS EQPT VENT AIR INLET VALVE is part of the cooling system for the aircraft's avionics (electronics).
It pulls in air to cool the computers below the cockpit.
The “CAUTION – HIGH AIR FLOW INTAKE” warning means that strong suction may occur while systems are running.

### Fly by Wire system (electrical)
Cables and Pulleys move stearing components.
Since 1930s there are fly-by-wire planes. Russian manufacturer Tupolev developed the first FBW System
